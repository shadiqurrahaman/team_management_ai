# 10-Minute Customer Interview Script

Goal: learn, don't pitch. Ask about **past behaviour**, not opinions about the future.
Record answers in `prospects.csv` right after the call.

## Open (1 min)
"Thanks for the time. I'm exploring a tool for agencies and want to learn how you work
today. No pitch, just questions."

## Current use (3 min)
1. How does your team use AI today? Which tools, and who uses them?
2. Who pays for it? One account, or each person their own?
3. Roughly how much does the team spend on AI per month?

## Pain (3 min)
4. What's the most annoying part of using AI across the team?
5. Do you ever need to know how much AI time or cost went to a specific client?
   What do you do now?
6. How do you share good prompts between people?
7. Have you looked at ChatGPT Team/Business or Claude Team? Why or why not?
   **(This tests the biggest threat.)**

## Value (2 min)
8. If one workspace solved {their pain}, what would that be worth per month?
9. Would you try a paid pilot at {founding price} for your team?

## Close (1 min)
"Can I follow up with a short demo video when it's ready? Anyone else you know I should
talk to?"

## Signals to record
| Signal | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | Brings it up unprompted, has a workaround | "Would be nice" |
| Budget | Already pays for AI tools | Uses only free tiers |
| Commitment | Agrees to a pilot or payment | "Let me know when it's ready" |
| Threat check | "Team plans don't track per client" | "We'd just buy ChatGPT Team" |
