# Outreach Messages

Rules: short, personal, one ask. Add a 30–60 s personal video when you can. Replace
`{…}` before sending.

## 1. LinkedIn / Facebook DM (warm contact)

> Hi {first_name}, quick one. I'm building a shared AI workspace for agencies: one
> bill for the team, ready-made marketing prompts, and AI cost tracked per client.
> Before I build more, I'm talking to a few agency owners. Could I show you the idea in
> 10 minutes this week? I'd mostly like to hear how {company} uses AI today.

## 2. WhatsApp (people you know well)

> Hey {first_name}! I'm working on a new tool for agencies: team AI access with cost
> tracking per client. Can I steal 10 minutes to ask how your team uses ChatGPT/Claude
> now? Your honest feedback would help a lot 🙏

## 3. Cold email

**Subject:** AI cost per client at {company}?

> Hi {first_name},
>
> Most agencies I talk to pay for AI per person and can't see which client the cost
> belongs to.
>
> I'm building a shared AI workspace for agencies that fixes that: one bill, spending
> limits per person, shared prompt templates, and usage tracked per client.
>
> Would you be open to a 10-minute call so I can learn how {company} uses AI today? No
> pitch, just questions.
>
> Thanks,
> {your_name}
> GrowMos

## 4. Follow-up (3 days later, no reply)

> Hi {first_name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to keep it to 10
> minutes, or you can reply with one line: what's the most annoying part of using AI
> across your team?

## 5. After a positive call: pilot offer

> Thanks for the time today, {first_name}. As discussed: I'll set up a private workspace
> for {company} with {n} seats, your client list, and starter prompts for {use_case}.
> Pilot price: {price}/month (founding-customer rate, locked for 6 months). Want me to
> send the payment link so we can start {date}?
