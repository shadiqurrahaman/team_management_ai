## Hero
**Headline:** One AI workspace for your whole agency.
**Subheadline:** Ready-made marketing prompts, one bill for the team, and AI cost
tracked per client, so you finally know what to bill back.
**CTA button:** Join the founding waitlist

## Problem
- Everyone pays for their own AI account. Bills are everywhere.
- You can't see which client the AI cost belongs to.
- Your best prompts are stuck in one person's chat history.

## Solution (3 blocks)
1. **Per-client tracking:** tag every chat to a client and see cost by client and by
   team member.
2. **Shared prompt library:** ad copy, audits and report templates the whole team reuses.
3. **One bill, hard limits:** prepaid credits, spending caps per person, top models in
   one place.
