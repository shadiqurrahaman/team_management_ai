# 06 — Go-to-Market (video-first)

## Channels

1. **Build in public:** short clips ("Day 3: building an AI workspace for agencies"),
   on LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. Each one is also a public deadline.
2. **Demo videos (60–90 s):** one problem solved per video, e.g. "track AI cost per
   client in one screen".
3. **Tutorials:** "How agencies can cut AI costs", "How to give a team AI access safely".
4. **Case study:** GrowMos as customer #1, with real numbers.
5. **Direct outreach:** short message + personal video to agencies in BD / UAE / AU.

## Weekly content cadence (minimum)

| Day | Output |
|---|---|
| Mon | Build-in-public clip |
| Wed | Outreach batch (5 messages + 1 personal video) |
| Fri | Progress post / demo clip |
